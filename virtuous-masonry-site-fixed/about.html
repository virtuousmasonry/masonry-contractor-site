About us

A family company with a twenty-year crew.

Virtuous Masonry LLC is family-run and based in Northern Virginia. The men laying your brick have been doing it for twenty years, together, and the person who quotes your job is on site while it’s built.

We are Class B licensed, carry general liability and workers’ compensation insurance, and put a written warranty on every completed job. We take a limited number of projects at a time so nothing sits half finished while we chase the next one.
